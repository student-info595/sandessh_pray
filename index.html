The Divine Presence

On the sacred occasion of Krishna Janmashtami, we remember Lord Krishna — the divine guide, the playful child of Gokul, the beloved of Radha, and the teacher of the Bhagavad Gita.

Bhagavad Gita

Krishna's teachings remind us to perform our duty with wisdom and without selfish attachment.

Hear the Divine Flute

Wisdom of Krishna

Do Your Duty

Focus on doing what is right and meaningful rather than worrying constantly about the result.

Stay Balanced

Success and failure are both parts of life. Keep your mind calm through both.

Choose Compassion

True wisdom is reflected in kindness, understanding and compassion toward others.

Be Human First

Before being divided by caste, language, wealth, status or differences, let us remember that we are human beings.

Krishna's wisdom teaches us to see beyond selfishness and hatred.

Humanity is our greatest dharma.

May Krishna's Wisdom Light Your Path

May your heart be filled with peace, your mind with wisdom, and your actions with kindness.

May we choose humanity, compassion and truth.
